# Growth Tracker

A personal growth tracker and helper, built from a weekly plan of small habits and exercises (each under 30 minutes).

- **Side panel:** jump between every section (slides in from a menu button on phones)
- **Today:** today's exercise with steps, today's events and next project steps, daily habit checkboxes, energy (spoons), mood, and notes
- **This week:** a habit grid, average mood, and gold-star count
- **Calendar:** month view with events (one-off or repeating weekly, monthly, yearly), project due dates, and the days you did your habits
- **Projects:** guided setup, one question at a time: ask why it matters at least 5 times, set a clear finish line, and plan the first 5 steps (30 minutes or less each). Your deepest reason shows next to every step. A focus timer for each step, celebrations when you finish one, and a gentle nudge after 7 days without progress. Built with autistic and ADHD brains in mind.
- **Hard moments:** a breathing coach (5-7-8, bee, box), 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, and quick tools
- **Helping others:** ways to support someone who's struggling
- **Sunday review:** weekly reflection questions, with past reviews saved
- **Backup:** copy your data as text and paste it back on another device

## Privacy and security

- **Passcode lock.** The first time you open it, you choose a passcode. You need it every time you open the tracker.
- **Encrypted entries.** Everything you enter is encrypted in your browser with AES-256-GCM, using a key made from your passcode (PBKDF2, 310,000 rounds). Only the encrypted version is stored.
- **Auto-lock.** It locks after 5 minutes without use, or after a minute in the background. There's also a Lock button.
- **Nothing leaves your device.** There's no server. A strict Content Security Policy stops the page from sending data anywhere.
- **Encrypted backups.** Backups can only be opened with the passcode they were made with.

If you forget your passcode, your entries can't be recovered. This repo and site are public, but they only hold the blank app, never your entries.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root).

Self-help, not a replacement for care. In crisis in the US, call or text 988.
