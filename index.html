Security

Your entries are encrypted on this device with your passcode (AES-256). The tracker locks itself after 5 minutes without use, or when you leave it in the background for a minute. If you forget your passcode, your entries can't be recovered.

Change passcode

Backup

Backups are encrypted too, so only your passcode can open them. Copy one now and then and keep it somewhere safe, like a note or an email to yourself.

Restore a backup

Paste a backup below (or load a backup file), enter the passcode it was made with, and press Restore. This replaces what's on this device.

Start over

Deletes every entry on this device. Copy a backup first.
